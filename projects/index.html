Research

Our faculty and students work on artificial intelligence, robotics, and computing education. Related papers are listed with each project.

On-going Projects

Robotic Control Reasoning with Locally-Hosted LLMs

A kinematic-semantic evaluation framework that connects the Webots physics simulator to locally hosted LLMs through Ollama, separating fine-grained robot controls from high-level natural-language commands. Led by Avery Leber (SURI 2026); the first paper won Best Student Paper at CCSC Eastern 2026.

Related publications
Evaluating the Robotic Control Reasoning Potential of Locally-Hosted LLMs Avery E. Leber. 42nd CCSC Eastern Regional Conference (Best Student Paper), 2026.
large-language-models robotics
Pushing the Limits of Local AI - Benchmarking LLMs for Code Generation

Extends our award-winning evaluation of locally hosted LLMs from eight open-source models to more than fifty (0.5B to 14B parameters), benchmarked on HumanEval, MBPP+, CodeContests, and curated Kattis and LeetCode corpora. Led by Kadin Matotek with support from SURF, the CSM Undergraduate Student Research Award, and SRCA.

Related publications
Evaluating the Limitations of Local LLMs in Solving Complex Programming Challenges Kadin Matotek, Heather Cassel, Md Amiruzzaman, Linh B. Ngo. Journal of Computing Sciences in Colleges, 41(3), 138–152 (CCSC Eastern 2025, Best Faculty Paper, Research), 2025.
FACE: A Framework for AI-Driven Coding Generation Evaluation Bao Ngo, Jack Formato, James J. May, Nguyen Ho, Hoang Bui, Linh B. Ngo. Journal of Computing Sciences in Colleges, 40(3), 263–276, 2024.
large-language-models
Dual-Model RAG for Grounded Student Support

A locally hosted, instructor-controlled Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework for course-based community chat that separates retrieval and context assembly from student-facing response generation.

Related publications
Dual-Model RAG for Grounded Student Support Linh B. Ngo, Logan Fani, Nam Vu, Nam Hoang, Samuel Luong, Si Chen, Md Amiruzzaman, Stefanie Amiruzzaman. 42nd CCSC Eastern Regional Conference, 2026.

TinyML Course Adaptation with Cyber-Physical Project Extensions

Adapting open TinyML course materials to local hardware and student preparation, with student-built cyber-physical projects covering sensing, model training, embedded deployment, and evaluation under hardware constraints.

tinyml embedded-machine-learning computer-science-education
ASL Recognition, Translation, and Game-Based Learning

Machine vision, CNN, and transformer models for bidirectional translation between American Sign Language and English, and a gamified vocabulary learning system for Deaf learners. In collaboration with Dr. Stefanie Amiruzzaman.

Bidirectional Translation of ASL and English Using Machine Vision and CNN and Transformer Networks Stefanie Amiruzzaman, Md Amiruzzaman, Raga Mouni Batchu, James Dracup, Alexander Pham, Benjamin Crocker, Linh B. Ngo, M. Ali Akber Dewan. Computers (MDPI), 15(1), 20, 2026.
An AI-Based Framework for Translating American Sign Language to English and Vice Versa Vijayendra D. Avina, Md Amiruzzaman, Stefanie Amiruzzaman, Linh B. Ngo, M. Ali Akber Dewan. Information (MDPI), 14(10), 569, 2023.

RAGTox: Toxicity Prediction using GraphRAG

Using knowledge graph-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG) to improve reasoning, explainability, and trust in LLM-based toxicity prediction.

Analyzing, understanding, and modifying the Real-Time Transport Protocol (RTP) to enhance its security and user access management features.
