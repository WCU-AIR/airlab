Congratulations to Kadin Matotek for receiving the West Chester University College of the Sciences and Mathematics (CSM) Undergraduate Student Research Award for his project, "Pushing the Limits of Local AI: Benchmarking Large Language Models for Code Generation." Kadin will be recognized at the CSM Awards Celebration in the Spring semester.
