At the 42nd Annual CCSC Eastern Conference, held at Bowie State University on October 9–10, 2026, Avery Leber won the **Best Student Paper Award** for "Evaluating the Robotic Control Reasoning Potential of Locally-Hosted LLMs." The paper builds an evaluation framework that connects the Webots physics simulator to local LLMs through Ollama, and finds that the Qwen2.5 3B and 7B models show the strongest robotic control reasoning across accuracy, efficiency, and latency tests.

The lab also presented "Dual-Model RAG for Grounded Student Support," with WCU student Logan Fani as a co-author, which evaluates a locally hosted, instructor-controlled Retrieval-Augmented Generation system for course support.

Congratulations, Avery!
