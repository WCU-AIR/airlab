The AIR Lab brought home two awards from the 41st Annual Pennsylvania Computer and Information Science Educators (PACISE) Conference, held at Shippensburg University on April 10–11, 2026:

- **Best Graduate Paper:** "Emerging Trends in Large Language Model Use for Undergraduate Introductory Programming Education" by Brian Kominick, Md Amiruzzaman, and Linh B. Ngo.
- **Best Faculty Paper:** "Institutionalizing Client-Based Capstones Through Career Services: A PASSHE Case Study" by Linh B. Ngo, Md Amiruzzaman, and Richard Burns.

Congratulations, Brian!
